## 第 12 项 C：讲一个没有达到预期的项目

状态：用户确认，已同步 Dev 与 ResumeDoc 全题参考答案；英文按中文翻译。未做模型生成测试。此前假设的 20/18/14 分钟、100 题与人工修改数量全部弃用，不属于个人经历。

### 已确认中文

我想讲一下在可之科技参与的 AI 作业批改产品。我负责 Android 和多学科 RAG 相关工作。

第一阶段的产品已经上线，一份作业几秒钟就能完成 AI 批改。但回头看，这个版本还没有充分实现我们希望提供的教学价值。

当时主要解决的是单次作业批改，没有把同一个学生历次作业中的错题持续归类、总结和分析。因此，老师能够看到学生这次做错了什么，却不容易进一步判断：哪些知识点反复出错、薄弱点集中在哪里，以及接下来应该优先辅导什么。

我认为，这个阶段做得不够充分的地方，是对批改结果后续怎么被老师使用，考虑得还不够完整。快速批改能够提高效率，但要支持针对性教学，还需要把多次作业的信息联系起来。

如果重新规划，我会在第一阶段就把历史错题的积累和知识点归类纳入设计，再逐步支持薄弱点总结，让老师能据此选择练习和辅导内容。验证时，除了看批改速度，也要看这些分析是否准确、老师是否愿意采用，以及后续同类题目的错误是否减少。

这次经历让我认识到，做 AI 教育产品，需要沿着老师的实际工作继续往后想：批改完成之后，这些结果怎样帮助老师做出下一步教学安排。这也是我后来思考 AI 产品时，会更关注完整使用过程的原因。

### 英文表达

I'd talk about the AI homework-grading product I worked on at Kezhi. I was responsible for Android and the multi-subject RAG work.

The first version was already live and could grade a homework assignment in a few seconds. But looking back, it hadn't fully delivered the teaching value we wanted.

It mainly handled one assignment at a time. It didn't bring together each student's past mistakes, group them or analyze the patterns. Teachers could see what a student got wrong that day, but it was harder to identify repeated mistakes, weak knowledge areas and what to focus on next.

My reflection is that I hadn't thought enough about how teachers would use the results after grading. Fast grading improves efficiency, but targeted teaching also needs information across multiple assignments.

If I planned it again, I'd include recording past mistakes and grouping them by knowledge point in the first-stage design, then gradually add summaries of weak areas to help teachers choose exercises and plan support. I'd check not only grading speed, but also whether the analysis was accurate, whether teachers used it and whether students made fewer similar mistakes later.

The lesson for me was to think further through the teacher's workflow: after the AI grades the work, how does that result help the teacher decide what to do next? That's why I now pay more attention to the complete user journey when thinking about AI products.
